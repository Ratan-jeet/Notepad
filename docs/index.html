Privacy Policy

App: Notepad: Notes, To-Do & Memo
Developer: rpchauhan.com
Effective date: 19 October 2026

1. Summary

Notepad is an offline application. It does not require an account, does not connect to any server, and contains no advertising. Your notes never leave your device unless you explicitly export or share them yourself.

2. Data stored on your device

  • Notes — titles, content, tags, and timestamps are stored in a local database on your device only.
  • Settings — theme, accent color, text size, and layout preference are stored locally.
  • PIN — if you enable the app lock, only a salted SHA-256 hash of your PIN is stored. Your actual PIN is never saved and cannot be recovered.
  • Biometrics — if you enable fingerprint/Face ID unlock, authentication is performed by your device's operating system. We never receive, access, or store any biometric data.

3. Data we do NOT collect

We, the developer, do not collect or share: personal details, contacts, location, photos, files, analytics, crash logs, or usage statistics about your notes.

4. Backup and export files

When you use "Backup", "Export", or "Share", a file is created on your device and handed to the app you choose (for example, your file manager or messaging app). We never receive a copy. You are responsible for where you store those files.

5. Permissions

  • Biometric — only to unlock the app, only if you turn it on.
  • Files (file picker) — only to let you restore a backup or import a text file you select.

6. Children's privacy

The app does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

7. Future changes

If optional cloud sync or any additional data collection is ever added, this policy will be updated before that feature launches and you will be informed in the app.
